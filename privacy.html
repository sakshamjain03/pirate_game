Pirate Empire — Privacy Policy

Effective: 2026-08-29 · Applies to the Android release of Pirate Empire.

Pirate Empire is a mobile-first empire-building game. Creating an account is entirely optional — the full single-player game is always playable without one. This page explains, plainly and specifically, what data the app collects, when, and how you can have it removed. It will be updated if that ever changes — see "Changes to this policy" below.

Short version: If you never create an account, nothing you do leaves your device — no server ever hears from the app. If you choose to create an account (to keep your empire in sync across devices), we store your email address and your gameplay save data with our backend provider, Supabase, so it can be restored on another device. That's the entire list — no analytics, ads, or tracking are ever tied to your account.

If you never create an account

Everything the app writes — your save file, gameplay analytics, and crash reports — stays on your device only. Nothing is transmitted anywhere.

  • Gameplay save data. Your empire's state (islands, fleet, economy, progress) is written to a save file stored locally on your device.
  • Local, on-device analytics. The app records basic gameplay funnel events (for example: a session started, a chapter began or finished, an island was captured) to a local log file on your device, purely to help understand and improve the game. This log stays on your device and contains no name, email, advertising identifier, or anything else that identifies you personally — and it is never linked to an account, even if you have one.
  • Local crash reports. If the app exits abnormally, a small report (what happened, when, and which engine version) is written locally so a problem can be diagnosed if you choose to share it. It is not sent anywhere automatically and contains no personal information.

If you choose to create an account

An account is only for keeping your empire in sync if you play across more than one device — the game never requires one. Creating one, signing in, or requesting a password reset is handled by our backend provider, Supabase. Creating an account collects exactly two things, nothing more:

  • Your email address, to identify your account and let you sign back in.
  • Your gameplay save data (the same information already described above), stored with your account so it can be restored on another device.

Your password is handled entirely by Supabase's authentication system using industry-standard hashing — the game never stores or has access to your raw password. Local, on-device analytics stay anonymous and are never linked to your account, per the same design used before accounts existed. No advertising SDKs, trackers, or third-party analytics services are integrated, and no data is sold or shared with anyone other than Supabase acting as our data processor.

Account deletion

If you have an account, you can delete it and all associated cloud data at any time:

  • In-app: Settings → Account → "Delete my account", behind a confirmation step. Deletion is immediate and permanent.
  • Without the app installed: email sj@passthebot.dev from the address on your account and ask for it to be deleted — this is handled directly, by hand, no automated form.

Deleting your account removes your account record and your cloud-stored save entirely. It never touches your local save — you keep playing on that device exactly as if you had signed out.

Removing data

  • Clearing the app's storage or uninstalling it (via Android Settings → Apps) deletes your local save file, local analytics log, and any crash report immediately.
  • If you have an account, use "Account deletion" above to remove what's stored with Supabase — uninstalling the app alone does not do this, since that data lives with your account, not on the device.

Children's privacy

Playing without an account collects no personal information from anyone, including children. Creating an account requires an email address, so Pirate Empire's account features are not directed at children, and account creation requires meeting the minimum age required by applicable law in your region and by Supabase's own terms.

Changes to this policy

If what the app collects ever changes, this page will be updated first, and the change will be reflected in the app's store listing at the same time.

Contact

Questions about this policy or the app's data practices can be sent to sj@passthebot.dev.
