# Art Direction

Version: 1.0

---

# Art Vision

Stylized.

Readable.

Timeless.

Colorful.

Adventurous.

Gameplay readability always takes priority over realism.

---

# Inspirations

Sea of Thieves

Bad North

Dredge

Polytopia

Wind Waker

We borrow inspiration—not assets or mechanics.

---

# Style

Low-poly 3D.

Simple geometry.

Strong silhouettes.

Limited texture detail.

Bright oceans.

Readable islands.

Distinct landmarks.

---

# Color Palette

Ocean

Bright blues and turquoise.

Land

Warm greens.

Sand

Golden.

Enemy

High contrast.

Bosses

Immediately recognizable.

---

# Buildings

Buildings should visually evolve.

Camp

↓

Village

↓

Port

↓

Fortress

Players should immediately recognize progression.

---

# Ships

Every ship should have a unique silhouette.

Players should identify ship classes without reading text.

---

# UI

Minimal.

Large buttons.

Readable fonts.

High contrast.

One primary action per screen.

---

# Animation

Exaggerated.

Readable.

Responsive.

Avoid realistic physics when it harms gameplay clarity.

---

# Effects

Big cannon impacts.

Large water splashes.

Visible explosions.

Rewarding loot effects.

Feedback should be immediate.
